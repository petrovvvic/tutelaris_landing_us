The loop closes, even with a no.

A measure is created for a report or a whole pattern, with an owner, a deadline and a status. Feedback to the people who reported is a field of its own.

  • One measure answers everyone who reported the same thing, without anyone becoming identifiable.
  • Even a no comes with a reason. Silence is the most common reason people never report again.

Why people report again

Reporting has to pay off.

Not through bonuses, but because it becomes visible that something changed thanks to your own report. Right after submitting, and again later once something has come of it.

  • The progress trail across six stages also shows where things are stuck. An honest interim status keeps trust.
  • “This came of it: …”: the safety officer’s sentence reaches the person who reported, without revealing who they are.
  • No leaderboards, no points for individuals. Reporting and implementing are rewarded, never a low accident count.

Every figure comes from real data in the application, nothing is estimated. Anyone reporting via the QR code without signing in sees this feedback right after submitting; the later progress is visible once signed in.
